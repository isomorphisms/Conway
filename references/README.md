# References and redistribution status

This directory is deliberately rights-aware. A work being readable online does not mean its chapters can be copied into this repository.

## The Symmetries of Things

John H. Conway, Heidi Burgiel, and Chaim Goodman-Strauss, *The Symmetries of Things*, A K Peters/CRC Press, 2008.

Relevant material includes the early chapters on symmetries, planar patterns, Conway's orbifold signatures, and classification of planar symmetry types.

**Repository status:** reference only. No chapters or figures are copied here. The publisher lists the book as copyrighted, and available copies carry an all-rights-reserved notice. I did not find an explicit open redistribution license.

Publisher page:
https://www.routledge.com/The-Symmetries-of-Things/Conway-Burgiel-Goodman-Strauss/p/book/9781568812205

https://www.mit.edu/~hlb/Symmetries_of_Things/SoTerrors.html

## Rule for adding source material

For third-party prose, figures, PDFs, or substantial excerpts, record the exact license or permission here before committing it. Public-domain or explicitly open-licensed material can be copied with attribution; otherwise link to the source.
